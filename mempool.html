bitmark-kernel / browser-node — mempool watch (Bitmark · BTM)

Watch the unconfirmed mempool for payments to a Bitmark address — in your browser, with no explorer API. This tab connects to a real Bitmark peer over the bridge, listens to the transaction relay flow (inv → getdata → tx), decodes each transaction itself, and flags any output paying your address. home · wallet · explorer

⚠ It watches the flow, it can't dump the pool. There's no "give me everything paying address X" — this catches payments as they propagate to us while the tab is open. Open it, then send the payment. (For confirmed history, use the explorer.)
