Headers are validated by the same HeaderEngine that validates Bitcoin, with btm:mainnet params and multiAlgoPow:true (PoW + difficulty + version rules skipped, linkage kept). The chain persists to OPFS, so it resumes across reloads. Proven in test-headers.mjs (2,000 real headers validated). ~2.4M blocks — full sync is slow; leave it running.
